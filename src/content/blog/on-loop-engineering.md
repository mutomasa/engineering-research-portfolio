Things are moving fast around AI Coding Agents. We've gone from prompt engineering → context engineering → harness engineering, and now there's also a technique called loop engineering.

This article from Classmethod explains it clearly: [dev.classmethod.jp](https://dev.classmethod.jp/articles/claude-loop-engineering-practice/)

This other article is also good for getting the general shape of loop engineering: [zenn.dev](https://zenn.dev/ino_h/articles/2026-06-16-loop-engineering-goal)

There's an Agent SDK as well (I believe Codex has something similar, though I don't know it well), and it looks like a fair amount of AI Coding Agent functionality can be implemented directly as a program. [code.claude.com](https://code.claude.com/docs/ja/agent-sdk/overview)
